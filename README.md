# Alan Gray | Fashion, from idea to form

I’m a fashion designer at Branch-Ellis. I’m interested in the decisions that turn a visual idea into a garment: silhouette, material, proportion, and finish.

For me, design is both expression and problem-solving. A piece should have a point of view, but it also has to work as something people can wear.

## Design practice
- Fashion and garment design
- Silhouette and proportion
- Material and textile selection
- Garment construction
- Fashion illustration
- Trend research
- Creative direction

## Current focus
Developing ideas into considered, wearable design at Branch-Ellis.
